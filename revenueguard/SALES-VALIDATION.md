# RevenueGuard — 30-day sales validation plan (2026-10-22)

## Hypothesis
Small B2B commercial maintenance and repair firms handling 20–200 invoices monthly will pay $149 for a 14-day, 50-invoice exception review when they can see a concrete sample and have unresolved payment-status or duplicate-invoice pain. This is unvalidated.

## Buyer and promise
Owner, bookkeeper, or office manager at a small B2B service firm. Pitch: **Find overlooked overdue invoices, duplicate invoice IDs, and payment mismatches before they distort cash flow.** No promise of recovered money. No debt collection.

## Offer
$149 pilot, up to 50 invoices, 14 days; CSV-only, no credentials; written exception report and owner-approved reminder drafts. Owner authorizes all communications. Scope, confidentiality, retention, refund and payment terms agreed before any data exchange. No private records in public GitHub issues.

## Four-week experiment
- Week 1: produce one transparent fictional sample report and a public offer page; interview five qualifying operators about invoice reconciliation and willingness to pay. Ask how they currently detect mistakes, what mistakes cost them, and who approves spending. Do not pitch before learning.
- Week 2: identify 20 carefully qualified firms, record why each fits, and contact only via appropriate channels with individualized, truthful messages. Track deliverability, replies and objections; no unsolicited bulk email.
- Week 3: conduct discovery conversations, offer $149 pilots to firms with confirmed need; seek two paid pilot agreements. No unpaid speculative build commitments.
- Week 4: deliver agreed pilots and measure false positives, time saved, buyer-reported usefulness and willingness to renew. Decide whether to continue, change target, or stop.

## Funnel metrics (real values only)
Qualified prospects | contacts made | delivered | replies | discovery calls | quotes | paid pilots | revenue received | refunds | hours spent | recurring conversions. Log dates and source evidence; do not count our own internal demo as an external sale.

## Decision rule
If 20 well-qualified contacts yield no discovery conversations, revise buyer/channel/message before building more software. If five conversations yield no interest in a paid pilot, revise problem/offer. If two pilots fail to deliver measurable administrative value, pause integrations.

## Compliance
US commercial email, including B2B, must comply with CAN-SPAM: truthful sender/subject, ad identification as applicable, valid postal address, working opt-out and timely suppression. Do not send campaigns until operational compliance is ready. FTC guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business . No automated collection calls/texts or third-party collections. Consult qualified Texas/US counsel for regulated boundaries before service launch.
