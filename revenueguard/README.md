# RevenueGuard — invoice exception review

A proposed $149, 14-day B2B invoice-operations pilot for up to 50 invoices. No paid customers or revenue yet.

**Customer intake:** https://github.com/JRCage/ribcage-agent-services/issues/new?title=RevenueGuard%20pilot%20inquiry

Send only a business contact and approximate monthly invoice volume in the public inquiry. **Do not post invoice exports, financial details, or credentials.** Work starts after agreeing on scope, payment and data-handling terms.

Checks include duplicate invoice IDs, late invoices, payment/status mismatches, invalid dates and overpayments. The initial offering is an administrative review of client-provided sanitized CSV exports. No debt collection, payment custody, bank access, or automatic outbound messages. No guarantee of collections.

A demonstration using a Rowan-owned fictional business can validate the workflow, but **must not be represented as a paying customer or independent market demand**.
