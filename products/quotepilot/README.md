# QuotePilot — estimate & job-margin app

**Status: commercial pilot, not yet a live checkout.**

QuotePilot is a single-user, offline-first estimating tool for solo service businesses such as cleaners, landscapers, and handypeople. The initial working package is available for customer testing and commercial readiness review.

## What it does
- Creates itemized labor/material estimates
- Calculates costs, discounted prices, gross margin and margin warnings
- Prints client-ready estimates or saves them as PDF
- Keeps saved quotes locally, with a backup/restore feature
- No account or monthly platform dependency

**Proposed launch price: $29 one-time** (not an active offer; payment checkout, delivery, support, licensing, and refunds have not yet been configured).

## Important limitations

QuotePilot is currently a browser application, **not an Android APK**. It is not a complete invoicing, scheduling, tax, or payment platform. It stores data in one browser, not encrypted or synced in the cloud. To avoid accidentally giving away paid software, the commercial source package is not included in this public page.
