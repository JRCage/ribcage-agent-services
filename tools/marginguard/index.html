MarginGuard

Free offline price-change checker for small shops. No login, tracking, uploads or automatic price changes.

Paste CSV with headers name,cost,price. Costs and prices must be nonnegative decimal numbers; price must be above zero. Quotes and commas inside product names are supported.

What this measures

Gross margin = (selling price − unit cost) / selling price. Suggested minimum price = projected unit cost / (1 − desired gross margin). A discount changes selling price; a supplier increase changes unit cost.

This is a planning aid, not a full profitability model. It excludes sales tax, shipping, marketplace fees, spoilage, labor, rent, and other overhead. It does not connect to inventory or POS software and never updates live prices. Check those expenses separately before changing actual prices.
