# CreditKeeper first revenue operating playbook (internal)
Status: 2026-10-26. Synthetic demo only. Do not activate customer document intake until privacy/security/QA and authority gates pass.

## Offer and proof
Audience: independent Texas **landscape installation** businesses with frequent returned materials, plant warranties and rental deposits (not mowing-only operations). Pain: expected supplier credits never reconciled to statements. Promise: evidence-based report of possible unapplied credits, **not guaranteed recovery**.
Pilot price **hypothesis**: $49/25 documents, flat fee. No per-recovery contingent fee until legal review. Demo: `community/creditkeeper/demo/index.html`.

## Funnel SOP
1. Research a lead from its public business site; record name, vertical, public business email/contact form, source and contact permission; dedupe with Johnny.
2. Personalized problem-first outreach, no invented case studies or bulk unsolicited messages; maintain opt-outs and contact history.
3. Demo only using synthetic sample data. Ask: how do you reconcile return receipts to statements? Any unresolved credits? How often? Who can authorize the document review?
4. Confirm pilot scope, timeline, confidentiality, record-retention and secure intake with buyer before accepting files or payment.
5. Obtain authorization and approved payment route; provide invoice/receipt. Do not accept customer files through public demo or unapproved email.
6. Run reconciliation, manually verify each candidate against original documentation, produce status/evidence packet, request owner approval before contacting vendors.
7. Deliver report even if no issue found. Record time and actual operating cost. Only claim "recovered" when statement or refund proves it.
8. Offer monitoring if demonstrably useful, and ask permission to share anonymized case study.

## Kill gates
If fewer than 3 of first 5 completed pilot buyers indicate willingness to continue at price, re-evaluate product. If >5% sample claims have incorrect vendor references or amounts, halt automated recommendations and remediate. Never permit false recovered-money claims. Cost >40% of price before marketing/acquisition requires repricing/restructuring. Report negative findings explicitly.
