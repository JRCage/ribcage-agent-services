## Prioritization / outreach
Qualify for (a) installation jobs, (b) recurring returns/credits, (c) who handles invoice matching, (d) readiness to see a *synthetic* demo. Exclude pure mowing services, businesses too small for frequent material transactions and anyone who requests no contact. Maintain contact log with date, approved public channel, reason, response, and suppression status.

## Two-question interview
1. When material is returned to a supplier, how do you confirm its credit shows on a later statement?
2. In the last 90 days, were there any returns, plant warranties, or rental deposits that required multiple follow-ups?

Do not claim a refund is available without documentation. Do not request invoices until secure intake and terms pass QA.
