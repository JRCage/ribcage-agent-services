# Potential project: custom house and lot advisor

Status: BACKLOG / NOT ACTIVE. Added October 27, 2026 at the owner's request. Continue current revenue work; this entry does not authorize a build, new spending, partner contact or launch.

## Owner's concept
Connect a prospective homeowner's requirements with address (or ZIP/city/county) information: local building requirements, city/county lot records, dimensions and preliminary buildable envelope, plus winter/summer light and climate. Produce practical criteria for searching premade architectural plans. For a couple of dollars, offer a shortlist of plans from major house-plan sites matching the household, lot, orientation and region. Preferred outcome: operate long enough to demonstrate value, then sell the product to a suitable larger house-plan company.

## Proposed scope if promoted to active work
Start with San Antonio city limits and simple lots, then expand only with reliable sourced coverage. Separate address/parcel-specific facts from ZIP/city regional guidance. Label sourced, user-provided, estimated and unresolved facts. Keep zoning/footprint fit distinct from building-code compliance. Do not imply GIS boundaries are surveys or a plan is permit-approved. Account for unresolved easements, overlays, access, lot coverage, slope, flood constraints and private restrictions. Seasonal sun paths do not establish indoor light without windows and obstructions.

Deliver household criteria, preliminary footprint constraints, orientation tradeoffs, and 3–5 explained plan matches with exclusions and next checks. Use authorized plan metadata/images and transferable catalog permissions. An embeddable matching engine is the proposed acquisition/licensing asset.
